# Phase 0 — Policy drafts (⚠️ GET UK/NG LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE LAUNCH)

These are starting drafts, not legal advice. Custom garments, cancellation
rights and privacy differ between the UK (Consumer Contracts Regulations,
UK GDPR) and Nigeria (FCCPA, NDPR). A lawyer must sign off final wording.

## Terms (show before quote acceptance — QUO-03)

1. Dej. by Ayodeji creates bespoke formal attire to the measurements and
   design in the accepted quote. Each piece is made to order.
2. The accepted quote (scope, price, timeline, payment schedule) is the
   agreement. Changes need a revised quote accepted by the customer.
3. Prices are shown distinctly in GBP or NGN. No currency conversion is applied.

## Cancellation & refunds (show before payment — DSP-01)

- Consultations: reschedulable up to 24h before; missed appointments are chargeable.
- Production: once fabric is cut, custom work is non-refundable except for
  fault, misrepresentation or non-delivery, subject to statutory rights.
- Refunds, where due, go to the original payment method via Flutterwave and
  can take 5–14 business days depending on the bank.
- Nothing here removes statutory consumer rights.

## Privacy (ACC-04)

- Measurements, photos, briefs and messages are private to you, the studio,
  and authorised support staff. Never shared or sold.
- Payments are processed by Flutterwave; we never store card numbers.
- Data is kept only as long as needed for orders, accounts and legal duties.
  Ask for export/deletion at any time via the support form.
- Consent version and acceptance time are recorded at signup.

## Support & disputes (DSP-03/04)

- Raise issues via the in-app support form with photos/receipts. Every case
  gets a reference number and a written outcome. No outcome is pre-guaranteed.
